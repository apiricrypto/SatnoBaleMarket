# SATNO Brand

## Brand identity
Use the approved SATNO logo as supplied. Do not redesign, redraw, reinterpret, or substitute the logo unless the user explicitly asks for a new logo concept.

## Visual direction
- Clean, engineering-oriented, modern, and credible.
- Common SATNO visual palette: blue/teal with yellow or solar-orange accents.
- For technical backgrounds, dark navy/teal can be used with a clear central area for Persian headlines.
- Prefer strong hierarchy, restrained decoration, and readable Persian typography.
- Social Story deliverables commonly use 9:16 unless otherwise specified.

## Copy and typography
- Persian spelling and punctuation must be checked carefully.
- Do not invent company claims, certifications, project counts, addresses, or contact information.
- Use the company name exactly as provided for the specific artifact.
- When website/social identifiers are required, preserve user-provided forms exactly.

## Review checklist
Before finalizing, verify:
- logo is unchanged
- Persian text has no typographic errors
- company name and identifiers are correct
- hierarchy is readable on mobile
- brand colors are coherent
- no placeholder copy remains
